Can pretrained geospatial representations transfer across urban space?

Five frozen aerial, street-level, Earth-observation and location representations are evaluated on public-transport accessibility and building-energy prediction using spatially explicit validation.

Research design

Two tasks, one comparative framework

Interpretation

Predictive value was task-dependent

Urban form was observable

PTAL was strongly associated with information encoded in street scenes, aerial imagery and annual Earth-observation context.

Fusion was selective

Street-level and aerial information were complementary for PTAL, while DINOv2 and SatCLIP showed little additional benefit when combined.

Controls mattered for EPC

Construction age and floor area dominated EPC prediction. Representation fusion added little once these extensive property controls were available.
