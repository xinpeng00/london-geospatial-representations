Interpretive boundaries

What the targets represent

PTAL

TfL's Access Index represents public-transport service access. It does not directly measure destinations, travel behaviour or experienced mobility.

EPC

The current-energy-efficiency score is a modelled asset rating aggregated by postcode. It is not observed household energy use.
