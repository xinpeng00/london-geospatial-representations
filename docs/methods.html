Validation

Spatial separation is part of the estimand

Primary protocol

Five outer folds hold out 33 borough-level groups. Three group-aware inner folds tune Ridge regularisation. Scaling, encoding, imputation and dimensionality reduction are learned within training folds.

Sensitivity protocols

Random five-fold validation tests inflation from local similarity. Five continuous geographic regions provide a stricter spatial-transfer stress test. Further branches cover PCA64, XGBoost, matched MLP/GATv2 and EPC reliability.

Scope: borough-held-out and continuous-region results measure transfer within London. They are not external-city generalisation tests.
Metrics

Performance and spatial residual structure

Outer-fold R², RMSE and MAE measure predictive performance. Paired fold differences quantify incremental value beyond matched controls. Out-of-fold residual Moran's I assesses remaining spatial structure.
